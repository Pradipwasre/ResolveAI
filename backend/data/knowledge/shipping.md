# Shipping and Delivery

## Delivery times
Standard delivery takes 3 to 5 business days in metro cities (Mumbai, Delhi NCR, Bengaluru, Chennai, Hyderabad, Kolkata, Pune) and 5 to 8 business days in other cities and towns. Remote pin codes in the North East, Jammu and Kashmir, Ladakh and the Andaman and Nicobar Islands can take up to 12 business days. Business days are Monday to Saturday, excluding public holidays.

## Express delivery
Express delivery is available in metro cities for orders placed before 2 PM. Express orders arrive the next business day. Express delivery costs Rs 99 per order and is free for UrbanCart Plus members.

## Shipping charges
Shipping is free on orders of Rs 499 or more. Orders below Rs 499 have a shipping charge of Rs 49. Cash on Delivery orders have an extra handling fee of Rs 25.

## Delivery partners
We ship with Delhivery, Blue Dart, Ekart and India Post. The courier is chosen automatically based on your pin code. You receive the courier name and tracking number by SMS and email as soon as your order ships.

## Delivery attempts
The courier makes up to 3 delivery attempts. If all 3 attempts fail, the package returns to our warehouse and a refund is started automatically within 7 days of the package reaching us. Shipping charges are not refunded for failed deliveries.

## Changing the delivery address
You can change the delivery address only before the order is shipped. After shipping, the address cannot be changed, but you can ask the courier to hold the package at their nearest hub.
