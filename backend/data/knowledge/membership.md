# UrbanCart Plus Membership

## Benefits
UrbanCart Plus members get free express delivery in metro cities, free standard delivery on every order with no minimum, early access to sales 24 hours before everyone else, an extra 5 percent cashback to UrbanCart Wallet on prepaid orders, and priority support with faster human handoff.

## Price
UrbanCart Plus costs Rs 999 per year or Rs 129 per month. New members get a 30 day free trial once.

## Cancelling membership
You can cancel UrbanCart Plus at any time from Account > Membership. Yearly plans cancelled within 14 days with no Plus benefits used get a full refund. After that, the membership stays active until the end of the paid period and is not refunded.
