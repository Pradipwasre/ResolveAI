# Damaged, Defective Items and Warranty

## Damaged on arrival
If an item arrives damaged, report it within 48 hours of delivery with clear photos of the item and the outer box. Damaged items are eligible for a free replacement or a full refund, including shipping charges.

## Defective products
If a product stops working properly within the return window, request a return and choose "Defective" as the reason. For electronics, our technician may call you to diagnose the issue before the pickup.

## Manufacturer warranty
Electronics and appliances come with the manufacturer's warranty, usually 1 year. After the return window, warranty claims are handled by the brand's authorised service centre. The warranty card and the GST invoice from My Orders are needed for any claim.

## Extended warranty
UrbanCart Protect extended warranty can be bought at checkout for electronics above Rs 5,000. It adds 1 or 2 years of coverage after the manufacturer's warranty ends. It cannot be added after the order is placed.
