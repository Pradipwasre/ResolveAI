# Orders, Tracking and Cancellation

## Tracking an order
Open My Orders to see the live status of each order. Order statuses are Placed, Packed, Shipped, Out for delivery and Delivered. You can also ask our support assistant on Telegram with your order id for the latest status and tracking number.

## Cancelling an order
You can cancel an order for free before it is shipped, from My Orders or by asking support. Once an order is shipped, it cannot be cancelled, but you can refuse the delivery or return it after delivery under the return policy.

## Order delayed
If your order has not arrived by the expected delivery date, it may be delayed due to weather, local restrictions or high volume during sales. If the order is more than 3 days late, contact support and we will raise a priority request with the courier.

## Order shows delivered but not received
If the order shows as delivered but you have not received it, first check with family members, neighbours and your building security. If you still cannot find it within 24 hours, contact support. We will investigate with the courier within 48 hours and arrange a replacement or refund if the package is confirmed lost.

## Missing or wrong items
If an item is missing or you received the wrong item, report it within 48 hours of delivery with photos of the package and the items. Our team reviews the photos and arranges a replacement or refund.
