# Account, Privacy and Support

## Updating account details
You can update your name, email, phone number and saved addresses from Account > Profile in the app or website. For security, a change of email or phone number needs an OTP sent to the old number or email.

## Forgot password
Use "Forgot password" on the login screen to get a reset link by email or an OTP by SMS. Our support team cannot reset passwords or see your password.

## Deleting your account
You can request account deletion from Account > Privacy. Deletion is completed within 30 days. Orders that are still open, refunds in progress and legally required invoices are retained as required by Indian law.

## Support hours and channels
Our AI support assistant on Telegram and the website answers 24 hours a day, in English and Hindi. Human support agents are available from 9 AM to 9 PM IST, every day. If you ask for a human outside these hours, your request is queued and a team member replies when the shift starts.

## Verifying your identity in chat
To look up orders, the support assistant asks for the email address registered with your UrbanCart account. This is only needed once per Telegram account. The assistant never asks for passwords, OTPs, card numbers or your UPI PIN.
