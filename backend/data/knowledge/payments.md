# Payments

## Accepted payment methods
UrbanCart accepts UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm and any UPI app), credit cards, debit cards (Visa, Mastercard, RuPay), net banking from all major Indian banks, UrbanCart Wallet and Cash on Delivery.

## Cash on Delivery
Cash on Delivery (COD) is available for orders up to Rs 10,000 on serviceable pin codes. A handling fee of Rs 25 applies. You can pay the courier in cash or by scanning the courier's UPI QR code at delivery.

## No Cost EMI
No Cost EMI is available on orders above Rs 3,000 with credit cards from HDFC Bank, ICICI Bank, Axis Bank and SBI Card, for 3 or 6 months. The interest is given as an upfront discount, so you pay only the product price.

## Payment failed but money deducted
If your payment failed but money was deducted, do not pay again for the same order immediately. The deducted amount is automatically reversed to your account within 5 to 7 business days. If the order is confirmed later, you will get a confirmation message and no reversal will happen.

## Invoices and GST
A GST invoice is emailed when your order ships and can be downloaded from My Orders. To add a GSTIN for business purchases, enter it at checkout before placing the order. A GSTIN cannot be added after the order is placed.

## Payment security
UrbanCart never asks for your OTP, CVV, UPI PIN or card password over phone, chat, email or Telegram. Never share these with anyone, including people who say they work for UrbanCart.
