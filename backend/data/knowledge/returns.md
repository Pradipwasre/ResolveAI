# Returns and Exchanges

## Return window
Most items can be returned within 10 days of delivery. Electronics and appliances can be returned within 7 days of delivery, only if they are defective or damaged. Fashion and footwear can be returned or exchanged within 15 days of delivery.

## Items that cannot be returned
Innerwear, socks, cosmetics that have been opened, personal care items, gift cards, perishable groceries and items marked "Non-returnable" on the product page cannot be returned.

## Condition of returned items
Returned items must be unused, unwashed and in their original packaging with all tags, accessories, manuals and free gifts. Items returned in poor condition may be rejected after quality check and shipped back to you.

## How to request a return
Open My Orders in the app or website, choose the item and select Return. You can also ask our support assistant on Telegram with your order id, for example UC-10001. A free pickup is scheduled within 2 business days. Self-shipping is not required.

## Exchanges
Fashion and footwear can be exchanged for a different size or colour of the same product, subject to stock. Exchanges are free, and the new item ships once the pickup is completed.

## Return quality check
After pickup, returned items reach our warehouse within 3 to 5 business days and go through a quality check within 2 business days. The refund starts after the item passes the quality check.
