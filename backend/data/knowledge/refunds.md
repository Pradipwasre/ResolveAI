# Refunds

## When a refund starts
A refund starts after a returned item passes the quality check, when an order is cancelled before shipping, or when a package is returned to us after failed delivery attempts. Refunds for damaged or wrong items are approved by our support team after reviewing the request.

## Refund timelines
UPI and wallet refunds reach you within 1 to 3 business days. Credit and debit card refunds take 5 to 7 business days, depending on your bank. Net banking refunds take 3 to 5 business days. Refunds to UrbanCart Wallet are instant.

## Cash on Delivery refunds
For Cash on Delivery orders, the refund is sent to your UrbanCart Wallet instantly, or to your bank account by NEFT within 5 business days if you add your bank details. The Rs 25 COD handling fee is not refunded.

## Partial refunds
If only some items in an order are returned, the refund covers only those items. If an order no longer qualifies for free shipping after a return, the Rs 49 shipping charge is deducted from the refund.

## Refund approval
Refunds above Rs 0 that are requested through chat are reviewed by a human team member before they are processed. You will get a message as soon as the refund is approved or if more information is needed.

## Refund not received
If the refund timeline has passed and you have not received the money, check your bank statement for a credit from "URBANCART". If it is not there, contact support with your order id and we will share the bank reference number (RRN or ARN) so you can follow up with your bank.
